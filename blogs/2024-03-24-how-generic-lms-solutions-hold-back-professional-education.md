Learning Management Systems (LMS) are the bedrock of modern training and education delivery. However, for professionals dedicated to professional education LMS and continuing education LMS, a critical question arises: is your current platform truly built for your unique needs, or is it an adaptation of systems designed for K-12 or internal corporate training? While a generic system might seem functional initially, it often leads to missed opportunities, administrative burdens, and ultimately, stifled business growth.
