Creating consistent course covers is one of the most overlooked factors in building a professional, high-converting online course library. If your store is pulling images from multiple sources, stock libraries, webinar screenshots, or AI generators, the visual inconsistency may be costing you sales without you realizing it. This 2026 guide covers the three most effective methods for unifying your course cover images, including the latest AI tools that have made the process faster and more affordable than ever.
